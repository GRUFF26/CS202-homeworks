Beginner's Guide to Freestyle Swimming

Equipment You'll Need:

  • Swimsuit
  • Swim goggles
  • Swim cap (optional)
  • Towel
  • Kickboard (optional for beginners)

    • Instructions:

  • Enter the pool safely using the ladder or edge.
  • Practice floating on your stomach to get comfortable in the water.
  • Kick your legs up and down in a flutter motion to move forward.
  • Use alternating arm strokes—one arm goes forward while the other pulls underwater.
  • Turn your head to the side to breathe, ideally every 3 strokes.
  • Keep your body straight and streamlined to reduce resistance.
  • Practice short laps and take breaks as needed.
  • Focus on rhythm: arm, arm, breathe — and repeat.
  • Finish your lap by gliding to the wall or standing up safely.

    • Tips for Success:

    Start slow and focus on technique. Consider taking a beginner swim class or watching video tutorials for better understanding.

    Enjoy!

    Freestyle swimming is a great way to stay fit and enjoy the water. Remember to practice regularly and have fun!

    Safety Tips:

  • Always swim with a buddy or in a supervised area.
  • Know your limits and don't push yourself too hard.
  • Stay hydrated and take breaks as needed.
  • Be aware of your surroundings and follow pool rules.
  • If you're a beginner, consider using a kickboard for support.
